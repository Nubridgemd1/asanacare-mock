Telepsychiatry serving all of Texas

Asana Care & Mental Wellness provides online psychiatric care and medication management to adolescents and adults throughout Texas — including Houston, Dallas, Fort Worth, Austin, San Antonio, El Paso, Arlington, Corpus Christi, Plano and Laredo — as well as California. As a board-certified Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner (PMHNP-BC), Asana Aruna offers evidence-based telehealth treatment for ADHD, anxiety, depression, bipolar disorder, OCD, PTSD, panic disorder, insomnia, women's mental health and mood disorders. All visits are 100% telehealth — confidential, convenient, and accessible from anywhere in the state. Now accepting new patients and therapist referrals across Texas.
