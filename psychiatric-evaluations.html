A Calm, Unhurried Start

What is a psychiatric evaluation?

A psychiatric evaluation is a thoughtful conversation and assessment that helps us understand what you are experiencing — your mood, anxiety, focus, sleep, history, and goals. It is the first and most important step toward care that truly fits you.

With Asana Aruna, MSN, APRN, PMHNP-BC, there is no rushing and no judgment — just a safe space to be heard, and a clear, personalized plan when you leave.
