About the Practice

You deserve to be heard, supported, and understood.

At Asana Care & Mental Wellness, care begins with listening. Asana Aruna is a board-certified Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner (MSN, APRN, PMHNP-BC) who provides evidence-based, personalized treatment in a warm and nonjudgmental environment. Whether you are navigating anxiety, depression, ADHD, trauma, or a mood disorder, you will find a steady partner in your care.

Every treatment plan is tailored to you — thoughtful medication management paired with genuine support, delivered entirely online so care fits your life.

“Healing begins when you feel truly heard. My role is to walk with you — at your pace, with care tailored to your life.”

With expertise across anxiety, depression, ADHD, trauma, and mood and sleep concerns, Asana builds each plan in partnership with you — honoring your goals, your values, and your story.
